# Events and conventions

## Weekly in the shop

**Friday night Pokémon.** Doors at 6pm, first round 7pm, $10 entry, four rounds
of Swiss. Prize support is packs from the current Scarlet & Violet set, three to
the winner and one to everyone at 2-2 or better. We usually get 16 to 24 players
and it is the busiest four hours of our week.

**Thursday One Piece locals.** 6:30pm, $5 entry, three rounds. Smaller and more
forgiving than Friday; this is where people try a new leader before they commit
to a deck.

**Saturday learn-to-play.** 2pm to 4pm, free, no experience needed. We keep four
loaner decks behind the counter — two Pokémon, two One Piece. This is where most
of our regulars started.

**Sunday Gunpla build night.** Second Sunday of the month, 1pm to 5pm. Bring a
kit, we provide nippers, cutting mats, sanding sticks and panel liner. No charge.
Entry Grade and HG kits are on the shelf if you turn up empty-handed.

## Monthly

**Last Sunday draft.** $20 including three packs, starts at 1pm, capped at 24
players. Sign-ups open two weeks ahead and it sells out most months.

**Manga book club.** First Thursday, 7pm. One volume a month, chosen by vote.
Members get 15% off the chosen volume. Recent picks: *Witch Hat Atelier* Vol. 1,
*Delicious in Dungeon* Vol. 1, *Frieren: Beyond Journey's End* Vol. 1.

## Set releases

Pokémon releases four main sets a year, One Piece runs on roughly a quarterly
cadence of its own, and Dragon Ball Fusion World adds two or three. Release
weekends run Friday to Sunday: a prerelease on the Saturday, a sealed event on
the Sunday, and pre-orders handed over from Friday morning.

These are our biggest sales weekends of the year outside December, and we staff
three people instead of two. Sealed product is also where we make the least per
dollar, so a release week that looks enormous on the sales screen is a thinner
week on margin than it looks.

## The convention booth

We take a booth at six conventions a year, all within a half day's drive. The
booth carries sealed trading card product, prize figures, Funko Pops, plush,
pins, posters and a small manga selection — not the full shelf, since what sells
at a convention is what somebody can carry home on a Saturday.

Typical convention weekend: load the van Friday morning, set up Friday
afternoon, sell Saturday and Sunday, home Sunday night. We run the same point of
sale at the booth, so sales, stock and loyalty points all flow back to the shop.

Booth staffing is two people minimum. One person cannot both sell and watch the
figures.

## Requesting an event

Local groups can use the back tables for a private event on a weekday evening at
no charge, subject to us not already having something on. Email us at least two
weeks ahead with the date, the group size and what you need the space for.
