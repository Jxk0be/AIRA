# Returns and exchanges

Last updated March 2026. Ask any staff member if something here does not cover
your situation — we would rather sort it out at the counter than have you leave
unhappy.

## The short version

Unopened items come back within 30 days with a receipt for a full refund to the
original payment method. Opened items can be exchanged for store credit within
14 days. Sealed trading card product, single cards, food, drink and clearance
items are final sale.

## Manga, art books and light novels

Bring it back within 30 days in resale condition and we will refund it. Cracked
spines, dog-eared corners and coffee rings count as read, not as resale
condition, and those go to store credit at our discretion. Special orders for
out-of-print volumes are non-refundable once the distributor has shipped them.

## Figures, plush and model kits

Boxes may be opened for inspection at the counter before you pay — just ask.
Once a figure leaves the shop opened, we can only exchange it for the same item
if there is a factory defect: a missing part, a broken peg, paint damage under
the blister. Photograph the defect before you remove anything else from the box,
because the manufacturer's claim process needs it.

Model kits with missing runners are replaced by the manufacturer, not by us, but
we will file the claim on your behalf if you bring in the box and the manual.

## Trading cards

Sealed product is final sale once it leaves the shop. This is not us being
difficult: we cannot resell a box that has been out of our sight, and neither
can anyone else. Check the seal at the counter.

Single cards are sold as graded by condition — NM, LP or MP — and are final
sale. If you believe a card was graded wrong, bring it back within 48 hours and
we will look at it together under the counter light.

## Snacks and drinks

Final sale, both because it is food and because a ramune bottle that has been out
of the cooler is one nobody else will buy. If something is past its best-before
date on the shelf that is our mistake, not yours: bring it back and we will swap
it or refund it, no receipt needed.

## Apparel

Unworn, with tags, within 30 days, for a refund or a size swap. We keep one of
each size of the house tee behind the counter for trying on, so ask rather than
guess.

## Convention purchases

Anything bought at our convention booth can be returned at the Market Square shop
on the same terms, and the con receipt is valid. We cannot process returns at
the booth itself — there is no register cash float for refunds there.

## What we cannot do

We cannot take back anything bought somewhere else, even if we sell it too. We
cannot refund to a different card from the one that paid. We cannot accept
returns without some proof of purchase, though a card statement or an emailed
receipt is fine.
