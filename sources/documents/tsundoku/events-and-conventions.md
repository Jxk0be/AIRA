# Events and conventions

## Weekly in the shop

**Friday night Astral Clash.** Doors at 6pm, first round 7pm, $10 entry, four
rounds of Swiss. Prize support is packs from the current set, three to the
winner and one to everyone at 2-2 or better. We usually get 16 to 24 players and
it is the busiest four hours of our week.

**Saturday learn-to-play.** 2pm to 4pm, free, no experience needed. We keep four
loaner decks behind the counter. This is where most of our regulars started.

**Sunday model kit build night.** Second Sunday of the month, 1pm to 5pm. Bring
a kit, we provide nippers, mats and sanding sticks. No charge.

## Monthly

**Last Sunday draft.** $20 including three packs, starts at 1pm, capped at 24
players. Sign-ups open two weeks ahead and it sells out most months.

**Manga book club.** First Thursday, 7pm. One volume a month, chosen by vote.
Members get 15% off the chosen volume.

## Set releases

Astral Clash releases four sets a year. Release weekends run Friday to Sunday
with a midnight opening on the Thursday, pre-release drafts on the Saturday and
a sealed event on the Sunday. These are our biggest sales weekends of the year
outside December, and we staff three people instead of two.

The 2026 schedule is Tidebreak in February, Ember Accord in May, Gilded Static
in August and Veil of Nine in November.

## The convention booth

We take a booth at six conventions a year, all within a half day's drive. The
booth carries sealed trading card product, prize and scale figures, plush and a
small manga selection — not the full shelf, since what sells at a convention is
what people can carry home.

Typical convention weekend: load the van Friday morning, set up Friday
afternoon, sell Saturday and Sunday, home Sunday night. We run the same point of
sale at the booth, so sales, stock and loyalty points all flow back to the shop.

Booth staffing is two people minimum. One person cannot both sell and watch the
figures.

## Requesting an event

Local groups can use the back tables for a private event on a weekday evening at
no charge, subject to us not already having something on. Email us at least two
weeks ahead with the date, the group size and what you need the space for.
