Direction B — “Ledger”

Denser and more structured. Warm paper, hairline rules, a serif for headings and a mono face for every figure, so a column of money lines up and a heading never reads as a row.

Who it suits

An owner who reads numbers all day and wants more of them on screen at once — the whole KPI line, three findings and the chart without scrolling. It signals “specialist tool,” not “app.”

What you give up

Calm. More rules, more type sizes and more information per screen means more to take in at a glance, and it is less forgiving if a page gets crowded later.

The tradeoff in one line

Information per screen at the cost of breathing room; it keeps the equity in AIRA’s existing look instead of starting the visual identity over.
