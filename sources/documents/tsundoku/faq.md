# Frequently asked questions

## Where are you and when are you open?

The shop is on Gay Street in downtown Knoxville. Monday to Thursday 11am to 7pm,
Friday and Saturday 11am to 9pm, Sunday noon to 6pm. We close for Thanksgiving,
Christmas Day and the first Monday in July for inventory.

## Do you buy single cards?

Yes, Tuesday through Saturday before 6pm, so there is time to go through them
properly. We pay cash at 45% of our sell price or store credit at 65%, and we
only buy cards in NM or LP condition for sets currently in standard rotation.
Bring them sleeved or in a binder; a shoebox of loose cards takes an hour we do
not have on a Saturday.

## Do you buy back manga?

Only complete runs of a series in good condition, and only for store credit. We
cannot take individual volumes — a shelf of volume fours sells to nobody.

## Can you order something you do not stock?

Usually. If a distributor lists it, we can order it, typically arriving in one
to two weeks. Special orders need a 50% deposit, which comes off the final
price. Out-of-print volumes we look for through the secondary market and quote
case by case.

## Do you hold new releases?

Yes. Our subscription box service holds new volumes of any series you subscribe
to for 30 days. There is no fee and no minimum. Ask at the counter or email us
and we will set one up.

## Do you have a loyalty programme?

Every dollar spent earns a point, and 250 points is $10 in store credit. Points
do not expire. Convention booth purchases earn points too if you give us the
same phone number.

## Can I play in the shop?

There are six tables at the back, free to use whenever we are open, first come
first served. We ask that you buy something if you are going to be there for
more than an hour, and that food stays off the play mats.

## Do you run tournaments?

Astral Clash tournaments on Friday nights, entry $10 with prize support in packs.
Casual draft on the last Sunday of the month, entry $20 including the product.
Both are listed on the events page and posted by the door.

## Do you sell gift cards?

Yes, in any amount from $10 up. They never expire and work at both the shop and
the convention booth.

## Is there parking?

Street parking on Gay Street is free after 6pm and on Sundays. The State Street
garage is a three minute walk and the first hour is free.
