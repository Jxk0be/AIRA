# Frequently asked questions

## Where are you and when are you open?

The shop is on Market Square in downtown Knoxville. Monday to Thursday 11am to
8pm, Friday 11am to 9pm, Saturday 10am to 9pm, Sunday noon to 6pm. We close for
Thanksgiving, Christmas Day and the first Monday in July for inventory.

## Do you buy single cards?

Yes, Tuesday through Saturday before 6pm, so there is time to go through them
properly. We pay cash at 45% of our sell price or store credit at 65%, and we
only buy cards in NM or LP condition for sets currently in standard rotation.
Bring them sleeved or in a binder; a shoebox of loose commons takes an hour we do
not have on a Saturday.

## Do you buy back manga?

Only complete runs of a series in good condition, and only for store credit. We
cannot take individual volumes — a shelf of volume fours sells to nobody.

## Can you order something you do not stock?

Usually. If a distributor lists it we can order it, typically arriving in one to
two weeks. Special orders need a 50% deposit, which comes off the final price.
Out-of-print volumes we look for on the secondary market and quote case by case.
Figures with a Japanese-only release run three to six weeks.

## Do you hold new releases?

Yes. Our subscription box service holds new volumes of any series you subscribe
to for 30 days. There is no fee and no minimum. Ask at the counter or email us
and we will set one up. Popular runs — *One Piece*, *Jujutsu Kaisen*,
*Chainsaw Man*, *Kaiju No. 8* — often sell out the week they land, so a box is
the only way to be sure.

## Do you take pre-orders on figures and sealed product?

Sealed trading card product, yes, with a 25% deposit; we allocate on a first-come
basis and we cap Elite Trainer Boxes at two per person on release weekend.
Scale figures from Good Smile, Alter, Kotobukiya and Megahouse are pre-order
only, paid in full, and lead times of six to nine months are normal for those.

## Do you have a loyalty programme?

Every dollar spent earns a point, and 250 points is $10 in store credit. Points
do not expire. Convention booth purchases earn points too if you give us the same
phone number.

## Can I play in the shop?

There are six tables at the back, free to use whenever we are open, first come
first served. We ask that you buy something if you are going to be there for more
than an hour, and that drinks stay off the play mats. Ramune is fine at the
tables; the marble lids are not, so bin them at the counter.

## Do you run tournaments?

Pokémon on Friday nights, One Piece on Thursdays, casual draft on the last Sunday
of the month. Entry and prize support for each are on the events page and posted
by the door.

## Do you sell snacks and drinks?

Yes — Japanese imports, at the front counter and in the cooler. Ramune in six
flavours, Calpico, Pocari Sweat, melon Fanta, Boss coffee, Pocky, Hi-Chew, Umaibo,
Japanese KitKat. If there is something you want us to carry, tell us; the snack
order goes in every two weeks.

## Do you sell gift cards?

Yes, in any amount from $10 up. They never expire and work at both the shop and
the convention booth.

## Is there parking?

Street parking around Market Square is free after 6pm and on Sundays. The State
Street garage is a three minute walk and the first hour is free.
