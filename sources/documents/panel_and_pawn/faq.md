# Questions we get asked

## What comes out on Wednesday?

New issues arrive Tuesday night and go out Wednesday by noon. If you are on a
pull list, your box is behind the counter from then until the end of the month.

## I have never played a modern board game. Where do I start?

Ask us, honestly — that is the most fun part of the job. The usual answer is
something that takes under an hour and explains itself in five minutes. There is
a demo shelf by the window and we will happily teach anything on it.

## Do you have a game for two players?

Most weeks we have a dozen. Two-player games are the single most common request
we get, ahead of party games and ahead of anything with a big box.

## Do you host game nights?

Two tables, free, open whenever we are. No sign-ups and no fee. Thursday
evenings are the busiest and there is usually somebody looking for another
player.

## Can you find an old issue for me?

Sometimes. Back issues we have are in the boxes along the back wall, sorted by
title. Anything we do not have we can look for, but a twenty-year-old issue in
good condition is a hunt, not an order, and we will tell you honestly if it is
not worth the price it would take.

## Do you sell supplies?

Bags and boards, comic storage boxes, card sleeves in a few sizes, board game
sleeves and dice. Not a huge range, but the things people actually run out of.

## Do you buy comics?

No. We take board games for trade-in, not comics — the shop is not big enough to
sort and grade a collection, and we would rather send you somewhere that does it
properly than give you a bad price.

## Is there a loyalty scheme?

Not a formal one. Pull list customers get 10% off trades of series they follow,
and that is the whole scheme.
