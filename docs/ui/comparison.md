# Before and after

The `before/` shots are the seven you took in step 0 — the specific things that
annoyed you. The `after/` shots are generated by `python tasks.py ui-shots`:
twelve screens × two themes × two sizes, full-page.

Pairing them honestly means pairing *complaints* to *screens*, because the
structure changed underneath. Nine destinations became five, and four of the
seven "before" screens no longer exist as screens.

---

## The seven complaints, one at a time

### 1. Navigation scrolled sideways and hid itself

| | |
|---|---|
| Before | [`before/mobile/scrollable-nav.png`](before/mobile/scrollable-nav.png) |
| After | [`after/mobile/light/home.png`](after/mobile/light/home.png) |

Nine tabs in a horizontal scroller, mid-scroll, with "Dashboard" gone off the
left and "Staffing" cut in half on the right — and nothing indicating either.

Now: five destinations in a fixed bottom tab bar, all visible, thumb-height, with
the active one marked by a filled icon, a heavier label *and* a rule rather than
color alone. The shop switcher left the top of the screen entirely, which gave
back about 56px on every page.

### 2. The conversation jumped when you opened History

| | |
|---|---|
| Before | [`before/mobile/history-moving-chat.png`](before/mobile/history-moving-chat.png) |
| After | [`after/mobile/light/ask.png`](after/mobile/light/ask.png) |

The history list expanded *inside* the page and pushed the conversation down and
off-screen, so tapping it lost your place.

Now it is a drawer over the content. Measured: opening it leaves the thread's
top and height unchanged (141px → 141px, 537px → 537px). Focus moves into the
drawer and returns when it closes.

### 3. Paging meant scrolling past fifty rows, and the item names were gone

| | |
|---|---|
| Before | [`before/mobile/scroll-to-paginate.png`](before/mobile/scroll-to-paginate.png) |
| After | [`after/mobile/light/stock-all.png`](after/mobile/light/stock-all.png) |

The worst one. A 736px-wide table inside a horizontal scroller on a 375px phone,
showing five columns of money and dates and **no item names at all** — every cell
unidentifiable — with Previous/Next below fifty rows.

Now the table is a table on a desktop and a stack of cards on a phone: item name
as the heading, everything else as labeled pairs. Paging sits above the rows as
well as below.

### 4. Headings looked like rows, and columns did not line up

| | |
|---|---|
| Before | [`before/mobile/hard-to-tell-dividers.png`](before/mobile/hard-to-tell-dividers.png) |
| After | [`after/mobile/light/stock-reorder.png`](after/mobile/light/stock-reorder.png) |

"Paper Lantern Books" was `text-sm font-semibold` — one notch above the item
names beside it, which is not enough to read as a divider. The "GONE FIRST" badge
sat inside the item cell at 9.6px, wrapping the name onto three lines and
knocking every figure out of line with its header.

Now each supplier is a section with a real heading on its own rule, "Gone first"
is a labeled badge under the name, and Order / On hand / At cost hold fixed
columns.

### 5. Rows started in different places

| | |
|---|---|
| Before | [`before/desktop/findings-different-widths.png`](before/desktop/findings-different-widths.png) |
| After | [`after/desktop/light/home.png`](after/desktop/light/home.png) |

The severity badge was shrink-to-fit, so "SHRINK" and "DEAD STOCK" pushed their
titles to different x positions and the list read as ragged.

Now the severity sits in a fixed-width rail and every title starts at the same x
— measured at 442px across all rows, identical in both themes. On a phone the
rail collapses and the severity goes above the title, because 104px of a 375px
screen is too much to spend on an alignment you cannot perceive there.

### 6. Caveats were louder than the numbers

| | |
|---|---|
| Before | [`before/mobile/dashboard-chart.png`](before/mobile/dashboard-chart.png) |
| After | [`after/mobile/light/home.png`](after/mobile/light/home.png) |

A four-line yellow block sat between the KPIs and the chart, physically bigger
than the figure it qualified — and in the other shot, itself cut off mid-sentence.

The honesty was right and the volume was wrong. Caveats are now a quiet line with
an icon, still always visible, never folded away.

### 7. Everything else was on the dashboard

| | |
|---|---|
| Before | [`before/mobile/dashboard-top-products.png`](before/mobile/dashboard-top-products.png) |
| After | [`after/mobile/light/reports-sales.png`](after/mobile/light/reports-sales.png) |

Top products, category mix, location and channel splits opened the day on the
first screen. They are a monthly question, so they moved to Reports › Sales, and
Home became actions first: what to deal with, then one number each for four
things, then one trend.

## Every screen, after

Light and dark, phone and desktop, in `after/<size>/<theme>/`:

`home` · `ask` · `stock-all` · `stock-reorder` · `stock-not-selling` ·
`reports-month-end` · `reports-sales` · `reports-busy-hours` ·
`settings-data` · `settings-documents` · `settings-notifications` ·
`settings-appearance`

Light is the default now. Every one of the "before" shots is dark because the app
followed the OS with no way to say otherwise.
