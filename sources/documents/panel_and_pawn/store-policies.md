# Store policies

Panel & Pawn, Knoxville. Comics on the left, board games on the right, one
register in the middle.

## Hours

Tuesday to Friday noon to 8pm, Saturday 10am to 8pm, Sunday noon to 5pm. Closed
Mondays. New comic day is Wednesday and the boxes are out by noon.

## Returns

Board games come back within 14 days unopened for a refund, or opened for store
credit if a component is missing or damaged. Shrink-wrapped games opened at home
cannot be refunded to a card — that is the one thing people argue with us about,
and it is the one thing we cannot move on, because a reshrunk box is a box
nobody will buy.

Comics, trade paperbacks and graphic novels are final sale. Back issues are
sold as graded and are final sale.

Anything defective from the publisher we replace from stock if we have it, or
we order a replacement at no charge.

## Pull lists

Free, no minimum, no deposit. Tell us the titles and we hold each issue for 30
days from the on-sale date. Miss three months without a word and we release the
box back to the shelf — we will always email first.

Pull list customers get 10% off trade paperbacks of any series on their list.

## Special orders

Anything in print from Diamond or Lunar, usually two weeks. Out-of-print trades
and back issues we source through the secondary market and quote individually.
A deposit is only needed on orders over $100.

## Game library and demos

There is a shelf of opened demo copies by the window that anyone can play in the
shop for free. Ask and we will teach you — most of them take ten minutes to
explain.

No table fee, no reservations, two tables, first come first served.

## Trade-ins

We take board games in complete, sleeved-or-better condition for store credit at
roughly a third of what we would sell them for. We do not take comics, and we do
not take anything with missing components or water damage.

## Payment

Cash and card. No cheques. Store credit never expires and is tracked by phone
number, so you do not need to keep the slip.
